YOUR FIRST DISCOVERY

Fractions are equal shares

Imagine a delicious pizza! When we split it into 4 equal slices, each slice is one quarter, written as ¼.

The bottom number tells us how many equal parts there are. The top number tells us how many parts we have.

1Numerator
parts we have
4Denominator

Remember: the parts must be equal for the fraction to be fair!
